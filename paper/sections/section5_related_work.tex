\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Noise injection and diffusion.}
Dropout established stochastic feature perturbation as a general-purpose
regularizer~\citep{srivastava2014dropout}, while diffusion models make repeated
Gaussian perturbation the central learning mechanism~\citep{ho2020denoising}.
Latent diffusion moves this process into an autoencoded representation to
reduce computation while retaining perceptually important information
\citep{rombach2022high}. Our question is orthogonal to the nominal noise
schedule: whether its \emph{relative covariance family} remains uniformly
controlled as representation resolution grows, and whether the encoder or an
explicit gate supplies that control.

\paragraph{Function-space probabilistic learning.}
Function-space diffusion and variational neural operators formulate generative
learning directly on infinite-dimensional spaces
\citep{kerrigan2023diffusion,seidman2023variational}; recent work also develops
well-defined regularized variational objectives for function-space Bayesian
inference~\citep{cinquin2025well}. We study a complementary object: the
resolution trajectory of finite noise-injection objectives, and a computable
criterion that determines whether a nominal perturbation remains the same
geometric intervention under refinement.

\paragraph{Neural operators and discretization.}
Fourier neural operators are designed to share parameters across
discretizations~\citep{li2021fourier}. That property does not by itself make a
stochastic training intervention discretization independent: a normalized
Fourier mask may add new noise mass whenever the grid exposes new modes. We use
operator learning as the controlled test bed in which the underlying function,
physical modes, and task remain fixed while only the cutoff changes.

\paragraph{Gaussian operator geometry.}
Gaussian equivalence theory and Hilbert--Carleman determinants identify
Hilbert--Schmidt perturbations as the natural domain for comparing
infinite-dimensional Gaussian measures
\citep{bogachev1998gaussian,minh2022kullback}. We use this machinery as an
explanation and turn it into a learning-facing theorem, matrix-free diagnostic,
and spectral gate. The protagonist is resolution-dependent training
instability rather than measure theory itself.

\paragraph{Spectral regularization.}
Spectral normalization controls a largest singular value
\citep{miyato2018spectral}; low-pass and low-rank methods constrain coordinate
frequency or finite rank. None of these summaries alone determines cumulative
square summability of a whitened relative covariance. Schatten-filtered noise
targets that quantity uniformly across discretizations. The matched-frequency
construction in \cref{sec:exp-matched-spectrum} holds the Fourier diagonal,
trace, and operator norm fixed while varying only the hidden multiplicity that
determines $\Ttwo$.
