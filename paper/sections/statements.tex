This work develops mathematical diagnostics and learning algorithms and does
not involve human participants, private data, or deployment decisions. We do

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

The experiment protocol fixes two suites, three result figures, one result
table, the same-source resolution ladder, primary and texture metrics, budget
grid, go/no-go rules, and final audit probes before the main runs. For every
reported result, the artifact will record the exact model revision, data-split
manifest, quadrature convention, covariance reference, probe bank, training
seed, raw output, figure-generation command, wall-clock time, backward
equivalents, and peak memory. Final audit probes are never used for training or
operating-point selection.
