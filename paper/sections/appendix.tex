\section{Proof of the Stability Theorem}
\label{app:proofs}

Let $\lambda\in[0,M]$ and $g(\lambda)=\lambda-\log(1+\lambda)$. Since
\begin{equation}
  g(\lambda)=\int_0^\lambda\frac{t}{1+t}\,dt,
\end{equation}
we have
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\lambda^2}{2(1+M)}
  \leq g(\lambda)
  \leq \frac{\lambda^2}{2}.
\end{equation}
Summing over the eigenvalues of $K_N$ and multiplying by $1/2$ proves
\cref{eq:kl-t2-equivalence}.

In whitened coordinates, write
$X=(I+K_N)^{1/2}\xi$ with $\xi\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$. Diagonalizing $K_N$ gives
\begin{equation}
  \ell_N(X)-\E_{q_N}\ell_N(X)
  =\frac12\sum_j\lambda_{j,N}(\xi_j^2-1).
\end{equation}
The terms are independent and $\Var(\xi_j^2)=2$, hence
\begin{equation}
  \Var_{q_N}[\ell_N(X)]
  =\frac12\sum_j\lambda_{j,N}^2
  =\frac12\Ttwo(N).
\end{equation}
The signed case follows by bounding $g(\lambda)/\lambda^2$ on the compact
interval $[-1+\delta,M]$.

\section{Frequency-Matched Controls}
\label{app:frequency-controls}

For completeness, we verify the identities used in
\cref{sec:exp-matched-spectrum}. A flat unitary has constant-modulus entries,
so each diagonal entry of $U\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)U^\ast$ equals
$d^{-1}\sum_j\lambda_j$. Both spectra in
\cref{eq:matched-spectrum-pair} have trace $\eta m$, giving the shared
diagonal $\eta/m$. Their largest eigenvalue is $\eta$, while direct summation
gives
\begin{align}
  \sum_j\bigl(\lambda_j^{\mathrm{stable}}\bigr)^2
  &=\eta^2\left[1+\frac{m^2-1}{(m+1)^2}\right]
    =\eta^2\frac{2m}{m+1}, \\
  \sum_j\bigl(\lambda_j^{\mathrm{unstable}}\bigr)^2
  &=m\eta^2.
\end{align}
Thus the stable family has bounded Hilbert--Schmidt mass but diverging trace,
whereas the unstable family leaves $\Schatten{2}$.

To match spatial-frequency appearance, let
\begin{equation}
  G_R=\operatorname{diag}\left[
    (1+\lVert k\rVert^2)^{-s/2}
  \right]_{\lVert k\rVert\leq R},
  \qquad s>1,
\end{equation}
and set $K_{R,d}^{(\cdot)}=G_R\otimes A_d^{(\cdot)}$. The two families have
the same Fourier diagonal and hence the same radial power summary. For every
spatial mask $M_R$,
\begin{equation}
  \hsnorm{(M_RG_RM_R)\otimes A_d^{(\cdot)}}^2
  =\hsnorm{M_RG_RM_R}^2\hsnorm{A_d^{(\cdot)}}^2.
\end{equation}
Any frequency-only control that retains a nonzero low-frequency response has
$\hsnorm{M_RG_RM_R}\geq c>0$ and therefore preserves the unstable family's
$\sqrt d$ divergence. The comparison matches Fourier diagonal, radial power,
trace, operator norm, bandwidth, low-frequency response, parameter count,
probe count, and compute budget. Closed-form spectra provide the ground truth;
Hutchinson probes only validate the matrix-free estimator.
